How it works

Live in 10 minutes.
No tech skills needed.

Answer 5 simple questions about your business and your AI team starts working immediately. If you can use Instagram, you can set up AIBeautyKit.

Four steps.
Then it runs itself.

Most beauty pros are fully set up in under 10 minutes. Here's exactly what happens.

01
⏱ 2 minutes

Choose your plan and sign up

Pick Starter, Pro or Agency depending on your needs. Enter your business name, specialisation and location. That's it — no credit card tricks, no hidden setup fees. You can cancel anytime.

02
⏱ 5 minutes

Train your AI team

Answer 5 questions: your services, your prices, your tone of voice, your typical client, and how you prefer to communicate. The AI reads your answers and adapts its style to match yours — not the other way around. You can adjust and retrain at any time.

03
⏱ 1 minute

Connect your tools

Link your booking calendar (Fresha, Vagaro, Booksy, Timely, Acuity, Google Calendar) and Instagram in one click. AIBeautyKit reads your schedule and starts managing it. No calendar? Use the built-in booking page included in every plan.

04
✓ Done

Your team starts working

From this moment, Aria handles bookings, Celeste writes content, Luna manages DMs, Maya plans campaigns, Sage tracks your numbers and Vivienne spots upsell opportunities. You only see what needs your attention — everything else runs quietly in the background.
